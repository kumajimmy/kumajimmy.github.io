ClipFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

ClipFlow is a desktop application that runs entirely on the user's own computer. It has no servers, no user accounts, and no telemetry.

What ClipFlow accesses

If you connect a YouTube channel, ClipFlow uses Google OAuth to upload videos you approve and to read basic channel and video analytics for your own channel. It requests only the scopes needed for this.

Where data is stored

All videos, transcripts, metadata, and analytics are stored locally on your computer. OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. ClipFlow does not send your data, tokens, or Google user data to the developer or to any third party, and does not sell or share it.

YouTube API Services

ClipFlow uses YouTube API Services. By using ClipFlow you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke ClipFlow's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting an account in ClipFlow deletes its token from your Keychain. Deleting the app's local folders removes all other data.
